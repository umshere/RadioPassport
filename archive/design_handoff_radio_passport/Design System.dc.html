Radio Passport · Design System v1

Signal & Stamp

One system to replace the Frankenstein: a nocturnal shortwave aesthetic. Warm charcoal, paper white, one coral ink. Particles are the imagery — no generated photos. See it live in the redesigned app.

01 · Logo

Radio Passport World · Live
The mark: a stamp that broadcasts Perforated stamp ring + transmitter dot + two signal arcs. It reads as a passport stamp at rest and as a live antenna in motion. Coral arc = you; paper arc = the world answering.

05 · Particles, not pictures

{{ artCanvas }}
Every station gets generative artwork A station's ID seeds a hue and a particle ring; playback drives the amplitude. This replaces AI-generated images entirely: zero asset cost, consistent identity, and it runs on a 2D canvas so it's cheap on mobile. Rules: max ~300 particles, DPR capped at 2, pause when the tab is hidden, respect a reduced-motion setting.

06 · Scale model

· Chips are shortcuts (near you, featured, recent) — never the taxonomy. The Atlas (region → country → top stations) is the canonical index for 100+ countries. · Language is a first-class facet: searchable, shown on every country row. · Big countries surface Top Picks + cities, never a 2,000-row list; small countries show their handful of stations flat. · The globe is a complement, capped at ~30 brightest signals; it invites, the Atlas and search deliver breadth.

07 · What got cut

· Retro tuner dial, neumorphic tab bar, purple glow shadows, beige Mantine theme — four styles become one. · AI-generated hero images → particle system. · Separate mini/premium/compact players → one dock, one behavior everywhere. · Kept: atlas → country → station spine, moods, favorites, stamps, search.
