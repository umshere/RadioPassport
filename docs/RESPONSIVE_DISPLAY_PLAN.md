# Elsewhere on narrow and unfolded screens

Use the available width and height. A narrow cover screen and an unfolded display are two sizes of the same listening session. Keep the station, audio, search, and open sheets in place through the resize.

1. At narrow widths, keep the Departures Hall and Desk in one column. Keep search sticky while browsing the Hall. Preserve space for touch controls and the dock, including safe areas in landscape.
2. When the Hall has enough width, put the arrival card beside the gates. Use two columns for the Desk at intermediate widths. On short, wide displays, keep the compact Keeper and avoid reserving vertical space for roaming. Allow the installed app to rotate.
3. Verify 320×720 and 390×844 cover layouts, 700×720 and 768×720 opened layouts, 844×390 and 1024×600 short layouts, and 1280×800 desktop. Resize a single tab in both directions. Check that search and sheet state survive, the transport stays accessible, and the last row can scroll above fixed controls. Check a station session with the same audio element before and after resizing.
4. On physical iPhone Safari and in the installed web app, verify keyboard focus and dismissal, safe-area insets, fixed-layer painting, and playback through rotation. Browser emulation covers reflow and app state. Hardware testing covers the actual keyboard, orientation, and compositing behavior.
