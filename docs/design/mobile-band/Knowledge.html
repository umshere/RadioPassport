What the desk knows, and when

Enrichment arrives in four stages, and the screen is honest at every one of them. Catalog facts are certain the moment you land; the title comes from the air; MusicBrainz adds verified relations; web knowledge only appears if it cites the page it came from. Facts become stars in the sky and lines in the folio at the same moment — one arrival, two places to meet it.

Never

A spinner or a skeleton row. A placeholder that promises a fact we may never get is a small lie. The stage before is a finished screen.

Never

An invented title. If the station is quiet the track star never wakes, and the folio says so in one line.

Never

An uncited claim. Web evidence without the exact URL it was read from is dropped before it reaches the phone — so every link can wear its domain.

Never

A reshuffle. Stars are seated once and pinned. Knowledge arriving late lights a new seat; it never moves one you were already reading.
