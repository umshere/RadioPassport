Laws

01

The bar carries the wordmark and the passport. Nothing else. Search belongs to the page, not the chrome.

02

Corners are square. The two exceptions are artwork (2px) and the play disc. No pills, no rounded cards.

03

Sections are separated by a hairline, never by a box. rgba(232,223,208,.10)

04

Lacquer is the landing color, and it appears once per screen. Foil is wayfinding, ether is live, dust is everything said quietly.

05

Every tap target is at least 44px, and the ones that matter sit above the fold. Cover, then act, then browse.

06

One element condenses on scroll: the cover, into a 44px strip under the bar. Nothing else in the frame moves, ever.

07

The band is the 44px nav; the dock’s 64px is reserved only while something plays. A quiet room holds open no empty strip, and Theater greys out.
